## Overview
CCTC’s hi-tech ceramic products have been extended to electronics, telecommunication, machinery, electrics, environmental protection, new energy, fashion industry, etc. Our main products include ceramic rods, resistors, MLCC, alumina substrates, GTM products, LED PKG products, SMD PKG products, Aluminum Nitride Substrates, ceramic parts for optical telecommunication, metalized ceramic components, artistic ceramic, electrolytes for SOFC, etc. The products have been greatly diversified and have become an important part of the global supply chain.
