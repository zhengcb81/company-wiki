Korrun founded in 2005, was listed on Shenzhen Stock Exchange on December 21, 2016 (stock code: 300577). Korrun is the first A-share listed company in China luggage and bag industry. Driven by the vision of "To be a great innovative consumer goods company", Korrun pursues "innovation" and strives to become a leader in consumer goods industry through adopting innovation in products, manufacturing, sales and management. Korrun actively explores global markets, builds a global team, and strives to become a "world-class" enterprise. Korrun values environmental protection and quality, and insists on creating high-quality products to maximize value for the society and win "respect".
